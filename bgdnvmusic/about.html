ABOUT ME

BGDNV has been making music since 2015. What started as a fascination with electronic music gradually became a constant process of producing, learning and exploring new ideas. From Slap House and Deep House to Tech House and festival-driven EDM, BGDNV's music reflects the different influences that have shaped him throughout the years rather than following one fixed formula.

Along the way, his music has reached more than 13 million views on YouTube, accumulated over 130,000 streams on Spotify and found listeners in more than 170 countries. His tracks have recieved radio play across Europe, including Germany, Spain, Greece and Switzerland, while his remix of "Looking For Love" also recieved support from Disclosure.

Releases such as "Losin'", "Crazy Game" with Johnny B. aka BeyBo and "Not Your Type" with BeyBo and Dom Yeetfield represent different sides of the journey from melodic and emotional tracks to club-focused productions and festival ready bangers. With releases such as "Falling Down" with Zentone and "Lichter Aus" with Mika KLZ aka Myka, BGDNV got international recognition from various radio stations across Europe.

After more than a decade of making music, the goal remains the same: keep experimenting, keep evolving and make music without being limited by genres or trends. Follow BGDNV's musical journey on your favorite streaming platform and social media channels to stay up to date with the latest releases and news.
